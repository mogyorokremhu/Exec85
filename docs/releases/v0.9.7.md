**Early alpha: website monitor (and, in this release, the photo and video gallery).** Try it in a virtual machine first.

> **Warning: the installer erases the whole disk you choose.** Tested in virtual machines and on one laptop, **not yet widely on real hardware**.

### What's new in v0.9.7
- **Website monitor** (Apps > **Website monitor**, or the `sitewatch` command). Add the addresses of websites you want to watch and how often to check (every 10 seconds to any number of minutes). When a site goes **down** or **comes back**, you get a message on your phone:
  - **Phone:** install the free **ntfy** app (ntfy.sh), pick a long secret topic name (letters, digits, - and _; anyone who knows the name can read your alerts, so make it hard to guess), subscribe to it in the app, and press **N** in the monitor to enter the same name. **T** sends a test message.
  - **Discord:** press **W** and paste a channel's webhook address.
  - A site counts as down after **two** failed checks in a row (one bad answer is usually a hiccup) and as up again after one good one. A page that answers with anything below 500 counts as up (even "not found" or a login page: the server is running); no answer, a timeout, or a server error (5xx) counts as down.
  - The monitor runs in the background as a service, even when nobody is logged in. Press **S** to switch it on or off.
- Plus everything from v0.9.6 (screen brightness) and v0.9.5 (the screen in a browser, the Paper fix).

### Still missing
Databases, print server, more game servers, scheduled backups, user management, an automatic-update option; widespread real-hardware testing.

### Requirements
x86-64, BIOS or UEFI, **1 GB RAM for the installer**.
